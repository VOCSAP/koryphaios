# Mobile companion (LAN access from a phone)

The **Companion** dialog lets the operator access the window from a phone,
**on the local network only**.

## Pairing

1. Open Companion and click **Start mobile access**: the app serves its UI
   over HTTPS on the LAN with a self-signed certificate.
2. Scan the QR code with the phone camera (same Wi-Fi network). The QR embeds
   a one-time pairing token — it is **consumed on first use** and only valid
   for this app launch; restart the access to pair another device.
3. The browser shows a certificate warning on first visit (local self-signed
   certificate generated by the app): accept it.

Closing the desktop app cuts the access. If the host becomes unreachable the
phone shows a "Host disconnected" screen with a Retry button; relaunch the
app, click Companion and re-scan.

## Device management

The dialog lists **paired devices** (address, first paired, last seen) with
per-device **Revoke** and **Revoke all** (the lost-phone kill switch).
Starting/stopping the companion and revoking devices are desktop-only,
physical-presence actions — a remote client cannot perform them.

## The mobile UI

The phone gets a touch-oriented view of the window: session/agent status, the
roadmap as swimlane columns (drag cards between statuses, with undo), and
basic interactions (paste into a session prompt, move cards). It is a
companion for monitoring and light triage, not a full replacement for the
desktop window.
